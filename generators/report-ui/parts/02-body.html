Step 1 · Research and context dump

What the business is, in one page

Emplowise HR Services is a remote HR team for growing companies in Pakistan. It is a service, not software. The client keeps their headcount and their systems. They gain a named HR owner, written scope and a predictable cadence on a published monthly retainer.

The commercial argument in one line

A growing company already pays for HR. It pays in founder hours, in a salesperson who is never reviewed, in a designer who leaves without a handover, and in a contract that cannot be enforced. The retainer replaces that spending with a named owner and a written record.

Scope boundary, stated in the same breath

Emplowise does not act as a law firm, does not guarantee compliance or hiring outcomes, does not take board authority and does not replace the client's management. Recruitment fees, payroll tax scope, legal qualifications, representative allocation and contract terms are confirmed in writing after the Free HR Audit.

Why EmploStart stays free

It is distribution and goodwill, not a lead magnet. It asks for no email address and uploads nothing. The bridge to the paid service is one line in the audit output: hiring instead of job hunting, run the free HR audit.

Five figures the market repeats and nobody has measured

[CANNOT VERIFY] Share of Pakistani startups with no HR owner

No dated public survey counts HR ownership in firms of 5 to 100 staff. Settle it with one audit field: hr_owner_named (yes, no, founder by default). Twenty audits give a defensible internal rate, labelled as such.

[CANNOT VERIFY] Contract misuse rate in small firms

The 88.4% informal employment figure is economy-wide and cannot be quoted as a small-firm contract rate. Settle it with contract_status and notice_period_weeks per employee, then report the share below the statutory floor inside the audited sample only.

[CANNOT VERIFY] Frequency of notice-period violations

No public enforcement dataset counts notice breaches by firm size. Settle it by comparing notice_period_weeks against four weeks for every permanent record, and by logging exit cases in the review pack.

[CANNOT VERIFY] Sales underperformance in small agencies

No Pakistan-specific benchmark exists for agency sales productivity. Settle it inside a client: quota, achieved revenue and performance_score per salesperson over two review cycles. Until then it is a hypothesis, not a statistic.

[CANNOT VERIFY] Cost of the founder acting as HR helpdesk

No dated study prices founder HR hours in Pakistan. Settle it with a two-week time log in the audit: founder_hr_hours_per_week and the founder's own hourly value. Publish the method with the number, never the number alone.

Step 3 · Fictional case study

Your HR person is costing you more than your payroll.

A written illustration of the mechanism, not a client story. It shows what a named HR owner would have flagged, in which month, and which document would have existed before the decision.

F

Fictional. Made up for this report. Bilal, Northline Digital and every person and number in this section are invented. No client data, no testimonial and no measured outcome is represented here. The arithmetic below is illustrative and is labelled as such wherever it appears.

The company

Northline Digital, Lahore

22 staff. Retainer clients in retail and property. Monthly payroll about PKR 1.95m. No HR system, one HR generalist, no review calendar.

The decision maker

Bilal, chief executive

Founder, seller and final approver of every hire. Answers people questions on WhatsApp at 11pm. Reads HR email last, if at all.

The influence

Kamran, senior salesperson

Hired at PKR 85,000 plus commission, inside the 50,000 to 100,000 band. Closest voice to the founder. Never reviewed. Shapes hiring, pay and promotions.

Month 1 of 6

Before Without HR structure

  • Contracts. Six people on verbal terms. Two offer letters promise one week of notice. One designer is in month seven of a three-month probation.
  • Sales. Kamran books revenue he does not deliver. Commission is paid on signature, not on collection. No quota, no review, no record.
  • Design hire. Bilal hires Kamran's referral over the portfolio the HR generalist shortlisted. The referral cannot run a client handover.
  • Promotion. A junior executive is made sales lead because Kamran vouches for him. He has never run a pipeline review.
  • Retention. Ayesha, the lead designer, asks twice for a scope conversation. Both emails are unread. She starts taking calls outside.
  • Compliance. No EOBI registration, no gratuity or provident fund clause, no harassment inquiry committee, no written termination process.

After With Emplowise running

  • Contracts. Written terms for all 22, category named, four-week notice floor, probation capped and confirmation dated. Contract Risk Score published monthly.
  • Sales. Quota, collection-linked commission and a monthly performance pack. The Performance Alert names the gap in month two, not month six.
  • Design hire. A scored shortlist and a work-sample task before the offer. The referral is assessed, not assumed.
  • Promotion. A written leadership criteria sheet and a 90-day trial with a review date. Influence is not a criterion.
  • Retention. The Employee Retention Index drops for Ayesha in month three. A named owner books the scope conversation within the week.
  • Compliance. The Compliance Checklist tracks EOBI, social security, gratuity, leave and the harassment committee, with an owner and a due date per line.

Flag 01

Contract Risk Score

Six verbal contracts, two one-week notice clauses and one over-run probation.

Flag 02

Performance Alert

Kamran at 38 of 100 against a 60 floor, with PKR 510,000 paid across six months and no delivered retainer renewal.

Flag 03

Employee Retention Index

Ayesha, the lead designer, on a falling index: no review in 14 months, two unanswered scope requests, flat pay.

Flag 04

Compliance Checklist

Statutory checks pass and fail against the seeded register.
