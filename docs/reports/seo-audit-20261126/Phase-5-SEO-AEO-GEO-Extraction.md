## 5.0 Global truthfulness rules (apply to every page)
- **Authors:** real, config-sourced people only (Muhammad Ali — Advisor; Abdul Hadi — Co-Founder; Samarah Nabil — Co-Founder/Senior HR Business Manager). Wire as schema `Person`/`author` **on `what-we-are` + guides**, not blanket on service pages (would be untruthful).
- **Dates:** publish `dateModified` only where a real change date exists (legal = 22 Nov 2026 verified). For other pages, use the **actual last-content-change date** going forward; **do not backdate/invent** (I-03).
- **`sameAs`:** **withheld** until real, owned profiles exist — footer socials are currently `href="#"` placeholders (I-04). Add `sameAs` only to the live URLs of Emplowise's own verified LinkedIn/X/etc. **No fabricated profile URLs.**
- **Rich results:** **no promises.** FAQPage is *eligible and already implemented* on `/faq/`; nothing here guarantees SERP features (I-06).
- **No invented** clients, results, testimonials, review counts, ratings, or pricing beyond PKR 40,000 / 75,000 / Custom (all pages). Scope items stay "confirmed in writing after your Free HR Audit."
